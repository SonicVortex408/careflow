# What TSH is

TSH is made by the pituitary gland in the brain. It tells the thyroid gland how much hormone to make. When thyroid hormone is low, TSH usually rises. When thyroid hormone is high, TSH usually falls.

# Typical range

Many labs use about 0.4 to 4.0 mIU/L for adults. Ranges vary by lab and can be different in pregnancy and in older adults.

# What results can mean

A raised TSH with a normal free T4 is called a subclinical pattern. A raised TSH with a low free T4 suggests an underactive thyroid. A low TSH can go with an overactive thyroid, but it can also be affected by illness and some medicines. TSH is often re-checked after several weeks before decisions are made.
