# Get emergency help now

Call your local emergency number if you have chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, sudden weakness or confusion, severe bleeding, or thoughts of harming yourself.

# Contact your clinician soon

Contact your clinician soon if your results were flagged as needing prompt review, if your symptoms are getting worse, or if new symptoms appear.

# Before your visit

Bring your lab report and a list of your symptoms, when they started, and any medicines or supplements you take. The question list in your PolyMarker summary can help.
