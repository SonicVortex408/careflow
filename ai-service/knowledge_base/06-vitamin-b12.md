# What vitamin B12 does

Vitamin B12 helps make red blood cells and keeps nerves healthy.

# Levels

Serum B12 below about 200 pg/mL (148 pmol/L) is often used to suggest low B12. Values near the low end can be unclear, and clinicians sometimes order extra tests to check.

# Symptoms that can be linked

Low B12 can go with tiredness, weakness, trouble thinking clearly, and numbness or tingling. These symptoms have many other causes too. Low B12 is more common in older adults, people who eat no animal foods, and people with some stomach or bowel conditions or on some medicines.
