# What anti-TPO is

TPO antibodies are proteins made by the immune system that target an enzyme in the thyroid. They are a marker of autoimmune thyroid disease, such as Hashimoto's thyroiditis.

# Typical cut-off

Many assays use a cut-off near 35 IU/mL; some use lower values such as 9 IU/mL. Check the range printed by your lab.

# What results can mean

Positive antibodies are common and many people with them have normal thyroid function. In people with a raised TSH, positive antibodies make it more likely that thyroid function will change over time, so follow-up testing is often suggested.
