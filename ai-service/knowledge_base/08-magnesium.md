# What magnesium does

Magnesium helps muscles, nerves and energy production work.

# Levels

A typical serum range is about 1.7 to 2.4 mg/dL (0.7 to 1.0 mmol/L). Blood levels do not always show how much magnesium is stored in the body.

# What results can mean

Low magnesium can go with muscle cramps, tiredness and weakness. Very low or high magnesium needs prompt medical review because it can affect the heart rhythm.
