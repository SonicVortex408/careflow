# Tiredness (fatigue)

Ongoing tiredness is common and has many causes: sleep problems, stress, mood, infections, medicines, thyroid problems, low iron, low B12 and more. Lab results can point to some causes but cannot rule all of them in or out.

# Brain fog

Brain fog means trouble focusing, remembering or thinking clearly. It can go with poor sleep, stress, low B12, thyroid problems and many other things.

# Hair loss

Hair shedding can follow illness, stress, childbirth or weight loss. It can also be linked with low iron stores, thyroid problems, and less often low zinc.

# Why we ask about symptoms

Research shows lab values predict symptoms better when combined with what patients report. That is why PolyMarker asks about tiredness, brain fog and hair loss.
