# What zinc does

Zinc supports the immune system, wound healing, taste and smell, and hair and skin.

# Levels

A typical serum range is about 60 to 120 ug/dL (9.2 to 18.4 umol/L). Serum zinc is an imperfect measure: it drops with inflammation and changes during the day.

# What results can mean

Low zinc can be linked with hair loss and slow wound healing, but these have many causes.
