# What a functional range is here

PolyMarker compares your results with a large test group. For each marker it finds the range where people in that group reported the fewest symptoms. We call this the functional range. It is often narrower than the lab reference range.

# Important limits

The test group is synthetic: it was generated by a computer to build and test this system. Functional ranges, percentiles, groups and risk scores from it are for research and demonstration only. They are not clinical guidance and have not been checked in real patients. Always rely on your clinician's reading of your results.
