# What free T4 is

Thyroxine (T4) is the main hormone made by the thyroid. "Free" T4 is the part not bound to proteins in the blood, which the body can use.

# Typical range

Often about 0.8 to 1.8 ng/dL, which is about 10 to 23 pmol/L. Ranges vary by lab and method.

# Reading it with TSH

Free T4 is read together with TSH. High TSH with low free T4 suggests an underactive thyroid. Low TSH with high free T4 suggests an overactive thyroid. A normal free T4 with a mildly raised TSH is a subclinical pattern.
