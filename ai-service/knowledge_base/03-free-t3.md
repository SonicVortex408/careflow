# What free T3 is

T3 is the more active thyroid hormone. Most T3 is made from T4 in body tissues. Free T3 is the unbound part.

# Typical range

Often about 2.0 to 4.4 pg/mL, which is about 3.1 to 6.8 pmol/L.

# What results can mean

Free T3 can be high in an overactive thyroid. A low free T3 on its own is not specific: it can drop during illness, low food intake, or recovery from illness. The ratio of free T3 to free T4 reflects how much T4 is converted to T3.
