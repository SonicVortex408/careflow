# What ferritin is

Ferritin is a protein that stores iron. The amount in the blood reflects how much iron the body has stored.

# Levels

The World Health Organization uses ferritin below 15 ug/L to show iron deficiency in otherwise healthy adults. Higher cut-offs are used when inflammation may be present, because inflammation raises ferritin. Units ng/mL and ug/L are the same.

# What results can mean

Low ferritin can go with tiredness and, in some people, hair shedding. Very high ferritin can have many causes, including inflammation and liver conditions, and needs clinical review. The cause of low iron stores matters and is something to discuss with a clinician.
